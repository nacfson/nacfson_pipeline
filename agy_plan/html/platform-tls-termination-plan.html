7. Preflight TLS rules

IDRejects
TLS-1Certificate, CertificateRequest, Issuer, Order, Challenge, TLSStore, TLSOption, or kubernetes.io/tls Secret in a project namespace
TLS-2Route without tls: {}, or with secretName/certResolver/store/options/domains
TLS-3Project route entry point other than websecure
TLS-4Route match without a literal Host(), or with HostRegexp
TLS-5Hostname not covered by the platform inventory (FR-010)
TLS-6Inventory inconsistency: duplicate or non-platform store, missing Certificate, default certificate set, duplicate hostname
